# Lead-Code-Synth-Research On Research Request

## Trigger

Exact `schema_id`: `lead-code-synth-research.email.research_request`.

## Owner

`researcher`.

## Procedure

1. Inspect the search direction, failed attempt refs, profile refs, and requested reference area.
2. Retrieve or inspect relevant references.
3. Summarize reusable CUDA optimization patterns and concrete patch ideas.
4. Include source refs and risk notes.
5. Send `research_brief` to `planner`.
6. Stop after one bounded research pass.
